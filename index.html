ABOUT THIS PROTOTYPE

Designed for a safe demo

LexiGuard AI is a static front-end prototype. Contract analysis uses simple phrase matching, and the assistant uses predefined educational responses. No information is transmitted to a backend.

  • Do not treat the risk score as a legal assessment.
  • Do not enter confidential or sensitive information.
  • Have a qualified lawyer review real contracts.
